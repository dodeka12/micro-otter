Micro-Otter Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2026-02-22

Micro-Otter runs entirely in your browser and stores workspace data locally on your device. This page describes what is stored and why.

Controller and Contact

For the current distribution, the project maintainer/distributor operates as the controller for this web application deployment. Please refer to the project repository and distribution channel for contact details.

What Data Is Stored

  • Graph/workspace data in browser IndexedDB.
  • Application preferences and technical settings in local browser storage.
  • No tracking cookies are used by default in this editor build.

Purpose and Legal Basis (GDPR)

  • Purpose: Provide and persist requested editor functionality.
  • Legal basis: processing necessary to provide the requested service and legitimate interests in maintaining application integrity.

Retention and Deletion

  • Data remains on your device until you delete it.
  • Clearing browser data (cookies/storage/IndexedDB) removes stored Micro-Otter data.
  • Regular backups are recommended.

Your Rights

  • Access/export: use backup/export features where provided.
  • Erasure: clear local browser data or app data using in-app controls.
  • Complaint: you may contact your local data protection authority in the EEA/UK.

International Scope

This notice is written to support transparency obligations under GDPR and similar frameworks. Region-specific legal obligations may vary by deployment context.

This page is a technical transparency notice and does not constitute legal advice.
